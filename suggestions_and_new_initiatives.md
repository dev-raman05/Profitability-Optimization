# Strategic Suggestions & New Initiatives

In order to optimize the Profitability Index (PI) and reduce revenue leakage, the following initiatives have been identified. They target key operational inefficiencies, such as cancellations, under-utilized capacity, and booking platform margins.

## Key Initiatives for Enhancing Profitability

### 1. Tighten Cancellation Rules on Booking Platforms
**Objective:** Reduce revenue leakage from unfulfilled bookings.
- **Estimated CapEx:** ₹15,000 (Legal/system adjustments)
- **Projected Monthly Incremental Revenue:** ₹25,000
- **Feasibility:** High (4/5)
- **Rationale:** Cancelled bookings represent an opportunity cost. Enforcing stricter cancellation policies on third-party platforms ensures partial realization of revenue and protects the Profitability Index.

### 2. Implement Dynamic Pricing Engine
**Objective:** Optimize pricing according to real-time supply and demand.
- **Estimated CapEx:** ₹100,000
- **Projected Monthly Incremental Revenue:** ₹45,000
- **Feasibility:** High (4/5)
- **Rationale:** Automatically adjusts ADR (Average Daily Rate) during peak times or local events to maximize Revenue Generated, maintaining a healthy PI.

### 3. Loyalty Program Enhancement & Direct Bookings App
**Objective:** Shift bookings from high-commission platforms to direct channels.
- **Estimated CapEx:** ₹80,000 (App Dev) + ₹8,000 (Loyalty Promo)
- **Projected Monthly Incremental Revenue:** ₹70,000 combined
- **Feasibility:** Medium-High (3.5/5)
- **Rationale:** Third-party platforms reduce the final Revenue Realized. Driving traffic through a mobile app and loyalty program improves the Profitability Index by bypassing middle-man commissions.

### 4. Increase Minimum Stay on Weekends
**Objective:** Improve weekend capacity utilization.
- **Estimated CapEx:** ₹2,000
- **Projected Monthly Incremental Revenue:** ₹6,000
- **Feasibility:** Very High (5/5)
- **Rationale:** Protects against isolated 1-night bookings that block multi-day weekend travelers.

### 5. Curated Bundles (Couple/Corporate & Room+Breakfast+Spa)
**Objective:** Increase Average Revenue Per Guest (ADRpG).
- **Estimated CapEx:** ₹145,000 combined
- **Projected Monthly Incremental Revenue:** ₹50,000 combined
- **Feasibility:** High (4/5)
- **Rationale:** Upselling auxiliary services before check-in guarantees higher baseline revenue and provides a competitive edge in luxury/business sectors.

### 6. Introduce Last-Minute Discounts for 1-Night Stays
**Objective:** Fill perishable inventory.
- **Estimated CapEx:** ₹5,000
- **Projected Monthly Incremental Revenue:** ₹12,000
- **Feasibility:** Very High (5/5)
- **Rationale:** Rather than leaving rooms empty, offering dynamic late discounts secures immediate Revenue Realized that would otherwise be permanently lost.

---
## Summary Recommendations
Focus initial efforts on **Feasibility Score 5** items such as *Last-Minute Discounts* and *Increasing Minimum Stay on Weekends*. These require minimal capital expenditure and immediately improve capacity utilization. The medium-term goal should be implementing the **Dynamic Pricing Engine** and **Loyalty Programs**, which provide the largest boosts to the Profitability Index.
