# CV Highlight Points: Optimizing Revenue Leakage & Profitability

You can copy and adapt these bullet points for your resume or CV. Choose the ones that best fit the role you are applying for (Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Engineer, or Product Manager).

## 📊 For a Data Analyst / Business Analyst Role
- **Revenue Leakage Optimization:** Analyzed hospitality booking data (130k+ records) using Python/Pandas to identify revenue leakage; introduced a Profitability Index to track realization efficiency, leading to data-backed strategy recommendations.
- **Data-Driven Strategy:** Evaluated 10+ business initiatives analyzing CapEx against projected monthly ROI, formulating a targeted roadmap for pricing dynamic optimization and cancellation policy restructuring.
- **Interactive Dashboards:** Designed and deployed an interactive Streamlit (and Tableau) dashboard to track Key Performance Indicators (Occupancy Rate, Profitability Index, Revenue) across 92+ properties and multiple cities.
- **Root Cause Analysis:** Investigated the gap between Generated and Realized revenue across booking platforms and property types, uncovering optimization opportunities in direct booking loyalty programs.

## 🛠 For a Data Engineer / Analytics Engineer Role
- **Data Pipeline Engineering:** Built an automated ETL pipeline in Python using Pandas to merge 5 disparate data sources (Facts & Dimensions), optimizing I/O by transitioning storage from `.xlsx` to `.parquet`.
- **Performance Optimization:** Refactored a legacy Jupyter Notebook data pipeline into a streamlined script, mitigating data leakage in missing value imputation and accelerating processing times.
- **Robust Feature Engineering:** Engineered critical business metrics including Advance Booking Lead Time, Length of Stay, and Profitability Index to supply downstream BI tools with standardized KPIs.
- **Data Quality & Leakage Prevention:** Audited ML imputation steps for target leakage, redesigning the missing-value handling (ratings) to rely exclusively on historical median aggregations by property and room category.

## 💼 For a General / Project Management Role
- **Hospitality Profitability Turnaround:** Spearheaded a project to minimize revenue leakage in the hospitality sector, focusing on the Profitability Index to align operational metrics with top-line business goals.
- **Dashboard Deployment:** Delivered a comprehensive BI solution tracking regional and property-level revenue realizations to equip stakeholders with real-time strategic insights.
- **Business Strategy Formulation:** Developed a strategic initiative matrix prioritizing quick wins (last-minute discounts) and high-impact structural changes (dynamic pricing engines) based on feasibility and projected ROI.
